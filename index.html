THE RESEARCH COMPILATION

The Mission Behind 'Archaeology of the Gospels'

For over two centuries, scientific archaeology in the Near East has produced remarkable discoveries—yet these crucial reports remain sequestered inside obscure academic journals, inaccessible paywalls, and specialized university libraries.

Our objective was to dismantle this barrier. By synthesizing archaeological publications, excavation reports, numismatic data, and historical cartography into an accessible yet uncompromised volume, readers can now inspect the primary material culture with their own eyes.

“Archaeology does not replace faith; it brings to light the real world in which the Gospels took shape. If so many places, events, and circumstances described in them find support in archaeological discoveries, should we not reconsider the historical credibility of the entire Gospel narrative with an open mind?”
